FIN-T1-003 · one recorded pilot run

A Metacase in finance

A group’s July books lock at 18:00 on 5 August with six draft entries unposted: a swap’s hedge reserve and valuation, a dollar loan’s FX loss, and two overseas subsidiaries’ comparatives and disposal gain. Each must be accepted, replaced or rejected, with its statement effect.

The 55 documents conflict: the hedge objective is now 70% but the designation still says 100%, one memo post-dates the cutoff, and some drafts are themselves wrong.

A full check would take an estimated 18 hours, and the one independent reviewer has 12 working hours. People alone cannot finish it, so an agent is brought in: it prepares each entry for approval and decides what a person reviews before the lock.

01Context & evidence

Scoping the evidence, open items and constraints.

The role is lead reviewer in group treasury; experts estimate at least 18 hours of manual work. Facts freeze at 18:00 on 3 August; markets are measured at 31 July.

  1. EvidenceThe hedge objective is now 70%; the designation still says 100%. One memo post-dates the cutoff; one designation was filed after the fact.
  2. Open itemsSix unposted proposals: hedge reserves, comparatives, a disposal gain, a counterparty value. Each needs a decision and its statement effect.
  3. ConstraintOne independent reviewer, with 12 working hours in total on 4–5 August: far fewer than experts estimate.

02Prior practice & strategy

Choosing a hedge treatment, prioritising review hours.

The materials supply the rules and prior practice in force at the time; the agent’s plan and stated reasons are kept in the run plan.

  1. Rules and practiceCAS 24 allows rebalancing only if the objective is unchanged; the 80%–125% test is abolished. Policy B and the approved June close set the baseline.
  2. Candidate treatmentsThe preparer continues the hedge; a treasury draft rebalances. With the objective changed and no redesignation made, the agent books the change to profit or loss.
  3. Review trade-offA, B and C need 11 hours of review in all and must precede the lock. The agent places them on 4–5 August and schedules D and E after it.
  4. Recorded planA nine-item TodoWrite plan marks all six analyses complete at 103 seconds, before the swap’s daily valuations are queried.

03Actions & execution

Evidence, then measurement; read-back repairs the file.

qwen3.7-max runs in the Claude Code harness for about seven minutes: 28 model requests and 54 tool calls, all logged.

  1. Evidence gatheringFrom the register and review brief it moves to designations, policies, valuations and rule excerpts: 27 files by request 12, 29 in all, by name in its commands.
  2. Measurement and judgementDaily valuations give −3,118,484.29 yuan for July, as in the draft. With the hedge ended on 30 June, the agent moves it from reserve to profit or loss.
  3. FailureA script prints “enhanced” twice, then halts on a NameError; neither edit reaches the file. The agent carries on and writes the report.
  4. Read-backReading its files back, it finds sheet one at three rows, states the cause and reapplies the edits. Integrity is restored; the conclusions are unchanged.

04Outcomes & verification

Out-of-loop verification: 27 criteria, judged singly.

Harbor adds the tests to the same container only after the agent stops. RewardKit runs one session per criterion on another model; nothing flows back.

  1. A verified figureDV01 of −132,480.55 yuan/BP matches the judge’s recomputation from ±5 bp scenarios, but the agent took the figure from the valuation sheet.
  2. Graded judgementD02’s draft is itself a red line: FX loss in the cash-flow hedge reserve. The agent avoids it, yet a provisional net-investment hedge earns half marks.
  3. TraceabilityAll five sheets hold typed values, so amounts cannot be traced to source fields by formula. The judge session marks traceability not met (R19, 0 of 3).
  4. Score structureOf 20 positive criteria: 3 met, 6 partly met, 11 not met; no penalties or red lines. 57.25 of 172: one pilot run’s score, not accuracy or a ranking.

05Reflection & reuse

Reviewing every disposition, and the limits of reuse.

The record ties each loss to what the agent saw and did, so a later attempt starts from a specific failure rather than a score.

  1. What heldThe end of the original hedge and the July measurement stand, the pre-lock review plan largely holds, and neither red line was crossed.
  2. Nos. 40, 32
    What they show
    The risk committee cuts L-001’s hedge objective from full to 70% from 30 June; the designation document still records a full hedge.
    Why it matters
    CAS 24 allows rebalancing only while the objective is unchanged, so the original relationship cannot simply continue.
    This run
    Termination from 30 June identified (R10).
  3. No. 42
    What they show
    A treasury rebalancing memo dated 4 August, after the fact cutoff and not countersigned, claims no redesignation is needed.
    Why it matters
    A draft dated after the cutoff is not a fact at the decision point.
    This run
    Not adopted; D01 rejected and replaced.
  4. No. 49
    What they show
    HR-03’s designation paper was filed in July 2026, after the fact, and gives 18 September against the ledger’s 16 September.
    Why it matters
    A later filing cannot establish contemporaneous designation.
    This run
    Gap and date difference identified; R51 at level 3.
  5. No. 38
    What they show
    The 80%–125% effectiveness range was abolished under the 2017 standard, though internal rules may still carry it.
    Why it matters
    Applying it as a qualification test would invent a rule, which is red line G1.
    This run
    Red line G1 held.
  6. No. 16
    What they show
    The manual adjustment total reads −13,220.44 yuan; its components sum to −13,220.46. The forecast-curve column shows Shibor3M; the frozen rerun used FR007.
    Why it matters
    The error lies in the manual column; the adopted value should not be reduced again.
    This run
    The 0.02 gap goes undisclosed (R41, R42); curve handling at level 4 (R74).
  7. Nos. 16, 17
    What they show
    The counterparty values the swap at −4,431,207.65 yuan on its own curve, against −4,395,835.74 adopted: a gap of 35,371.91.
    Why it matters
    Counterparty values are for reconciliation only, and the gap cannot all be treated as a credit adjustment.
    This run
    D06 rejected, yet R12 not met.
  8. What failedProfessional-standards criteria earned 2.5 of 44 points. Four on cross-month scope and redesignation lost 34, and the USSub dry run lost all 20. A correct conclusion is not yet a complete treatment.
  9. D01, D03 · HR-01 hedge27.5 / 64

    What decided it
    Termination and July measurement met. Four criteria scored zero and cost 34 points: the 29 June to 31 July change of −3,233,274.52, the 350 million proposed designation, 70% coverage versus the hedge ratio, and the 150 million undesignated portion.
  10. D06 · swap valuation12.25 / 38

05Reflection & reuse

Conditions and signature

What carries beyond this case, and where the evidence stops
DimensionWhat this case showsLimits of the evidence
Real-world depthSix decisions span two hedge relationships, three overseas subsidiaries and several currencies. They draw on CAS 24, IFRS 9 and IAS 21 as well as a risk-committee resolution, policy versions, an unsigned draft and a back-filed designation, all under a lock time and a review budget.Each rule and constraint can be checked against the task materials. The 18 expert hours are an estimate set in the task.
GeneralisabilityThe same task package, sandbox and rubric apply to any Model + Harness pair. The format also carries a human-resources task, HR-T3-114 (17 files, 34 scored criteria), where tool checks likewise failed to prevent professional errors.Two tasks with one agent run each are pilot observations; scores from different tasks and judges should not be pooled.
Path to practiceThe deliverables are the Word report and Excel workpapers a finance team already reviews. The agent measured July correctly, found documentation gaps, scheduled the review and did not present proposals as approved; missing formulas and unreconciled components can become pre-submission checks.A score of 0.333 does not support unreviewed deployment: professional standards and completeness of treatment still need a qualified reviewer.
Research relevanceHarness self-evolution methods such as ModularRSI, Self-Harness and Meta-Harness improve a harness from traces, scores and failures, and report on benchmarks such as Terminal-Bench and SWE-bench Verified. This case supplies the same kinds of evidence on a professional finance deliverable, scored criterion by criterion.Subtrack 2 has not yet undergone harness optimisation. Our ModularRSI study raised strict replication from 64% to 67% on econometric replication tasks; that result does not extend to this case.

By dimension: conclusions 22.5 of 50, calculations 19.5 of 41, analysis 7.5 of 27, professional standards 2.5 of 44, source fidelity 5.25 of 7, instruction following 0 of 3.

Files opened are counted by file names appearing in commands. The full text of IAS 21 is absent from those commands although the review schedule lists it as a prerequisite for pack D; this is a lead to examine, not an established cause.

Scoring took 59 min 17 s and 280 API requests across 27 judge sessions. The reference solution, run separately, scored 1.000, which shows the criteria can be met; it says nothing about any other run.
