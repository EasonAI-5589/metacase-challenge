Homepage presentation: all four crests have a 72px display height in a borderless
row below the action buttons. Full university names remain available as hover
titles and accessible image descriptions. A CSS window
shows the NTU shield from the original university logo without the adjacent
wordmark. The source image bytes are unchanged; the window preserves the
shield itself and its original proportions. SUFE uses the opaque charter seal above
(sufe-university-official.png): its original red-on-white artwork on a white disc
in both light and dark mode. The transparent homepage asset is retained for provenance.
The Tsinghua crest (tsinghua-pbcsf-crest.png, below) sits on the same white disc.
The homepage Team cards show crests only, in their original colors and one 64px
column: the NTU shield (the same CSS window on ntu-university-official.png), the HKU
standalone crest (hku-university-crest.png), and the SUFE charter seal and the Tsinghua
crest on white discs.
NTU and HKU retain their original colors, including white within their shields.
No source image bytes are changed.
