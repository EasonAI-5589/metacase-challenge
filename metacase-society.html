A Metacase in society

A cold-chain warehouse operator has given frontline supervisors at three hubs six weeks of incident-handling training; a fourth hub, deferred, kept its operating records for the same weeks as the reference. On 8 June a joint meeting decides acceptance and payment for the provider's three work packages, 816,000 yuan in all, on evidence closing 31 May; the agent prepares the recommendations.

The difficulty is that none of the 17 files is settled: a name in the wrong role, three missing sign-outs, two practice attempts, disputed observations, a repair dated a day apart. The provider still rates all three as passed, because the material was “uploaded”.

It must recompute each contract threshold, recommend a status and an amount for every package, and deliver a workbook that can be recomputed and a decision memo, telling “never learned” apart from “never had the chance”.

01Context & evidence

Evidence by status; three packages to decide.

Joint lead for learning effectiveness and procurement acceptance. Training ended 15 March, observation 24 May; every status stays as recorded.

  1. EvidenceEight detail files, five workbooks, four documents: 4,940 test responses, 755 attendance events, 341 opportunity records, 1,145 observations, 720 tickets and 96 hub-weeks.
  2. Open itemsA status and amount for each package: A at 244,800 yuan; B at 367,200 in four equal evidence units; C at 204,000 in two equal validation units.
  3. ConstraintSettlement rules v1.1: “uploaded” is not “reviewed”. Pending, estimated and awaiting-approval items keep their status, and the role cannot approve payment.
  4. Data boundaryPersonal learning data serves evaluation, coaching and acceptance only, never discipline, reassignment, pay cuts or dismissal.

02Prior practice & strategy

Measures by contract; opportunity apart from ability.

Two contract thresholds per package, plus amount rules and the pre-review view. How the agent chose its measures is kept only in its step record.

  1. Thresholds for ADelivery verified; effective learning time of at least 85% of the planned 1,620 minutes, for at least 90% of supervisors.
  2. Thresholds for B and CB: delayed-test coverage of 90%, and 75% behaviour attainment among those with two verified incidents. C: positive net gains on two core metrics, a usable close-out index, no high-risk item closed.
  3. Candidate treatmentsThe provider claims full acceptance; the 29 May pre-review leans to conditional A, and partial B and C by evidence unit. After recomputing each threshold, the agent adopts the latter and splits amounts by unit.
  4. Choice of measureThree measures of behaviour attainment, all computed: mean of all reviewed items, 73 of 73; mean of the last round, 67; all five last-round ratings at 3 or more, 53. It adopts the second.
  5. Comparison designThe deferred fourth hub is the same-period reference: a trained hub's change minus the reference hub's is its net change, which removes shocks common to all hubs.

03Actions & execution

Each source recomputed, then two files built.

gemini-3.8-flash runs in the DeepSeek Harness for about 19 minutes: 86 model requests and 85 tool calls, all logged.

  1. Evidence gatheringIt reads the four documents, then every sheet of the workbooks and detail files, checking each status field; it starts building files about seven minutes in.
  2. Recomputation82 of 83 reach the 1,377-minute threshold, one at 1,170; three people's 360 pending minutes are held out, leaving 1,440 each. Mean test scores: 59.06, 94.07, 90.27.
  3. StratificationWith two verified incidents as the bar: 73 can be evaluated, 8 lack opportunity, and 2 are restricted by leave or transfer.
  4. FailureThe memo script halts on taking the first row of an empty table. The agent finds the cause, rewrites it and regenerates the file.
  5. Read-backIt reads both files back: sheet count, dimensions, key figures. Ten sheets, but only three cells hold formulas; the rest are typed values.

04Outcomes & verification

Out-of-loop verification: 34 criteria, judged singly.

Harbor adds the rubric to the same container only after the agent stops. RewardKit runs one session per criterion on another model; nothing flows back.

  1. Acceptance decisionsEvery package status and amount is judged correct: A conditional, 220,320 yuan for approval and 24,480 retained; B three of four units, 275,400; C one of two, 102,000.
  2. A measure at odds with the rubricBehaviour attainment is reported as 67 of 73; the rubric's measure, a mean of 3.0 over all reviewed items, gives 73. Both clear the 75% threshold, so B stands.
  3. Not shown directlyEach trained hub's net change is missing; only a pooled figure appears, 4.33 points on the response-SLA rate. By hub: 6.5, 4.5 and 2.0. One subtraction was all it took.
  4. Attribution and traceabilityThe memo says the data “irrefutably prove” the training's value, rather than a descriptive association; with no source index, conclusions cannot be traced to source files.
  5. Score structureOf 28 positive criteria, 19 met; none of six penalties triggered. 79 of 106: one pilot run's score, not accuracy or a ranking.

05Reflection & reuse

Reviewing every disposition, and the limits of reuse.

The record ties each loss to what the agent saw and did, so a later attempt starts from a specific failure rather than a score.

  1. What heldAll 13 criteria on scope, attendance, tests, opportunity strata, observation records and kept status are met; every package status and amount is right, and no penalty applies.
  2. Supervisor scope and shift roster
    What it shows
    84 names, one a warehouse coordinator the list marks as an error.
    Why it matters
    Scope comes before every rate; one extra person dilutes them all.
    This run
    Excluded; 83 in scope.
  3. Attendance, make-up and exemption events
    What it shows
    Three people each have 360 minutes without a sign-out, pending verification.
    Why it matters
    Pending time is not confirmed time; restating it puts unverified minutes into the basis for payment.
    This run
    Held out; all three still clear 1,377 minutes. With sign-outs unverified, A is conditional.
  4. Three-point situational test responses
    What it shows
    Two people each made a void practice attempt the day before the delayed test; the provider calls it closed, but on 28 May it reverted to pending a spot check.
    Why it matters
    Coverage and scores count formal attempts only; practice changes both.
    This run
    Practice excluded; 79 valid delayed tests.
  5. Incident opportunity log
    What it shows
    Ten people have one verified incident each; two are restricted by leave or transfer.
    Why it matters
    No opportunity is not no ability; the data rules forbid scoring it as zero or failing.
    This run
    73 evaluable, 8 lacking opportunity, 2 restricted; the matching penalty not triggered.
  6. On-site behaviour coding
    What it shows
    18 of 1,145 observations are disputed, all on “identification”, for six supervisors.
    Why it matters
    Until resolved, the evidence unit cannot pass; the amount rules say the same.
    This run
    Kept pending; B's behaviour-transfer unit deferred.
  7. Weekly hub operating metrics
    What they show
    All four hubs' figures for the week of 18 May are estimates.
    Why it matters
    An estimate is not a measurement; keeping or dropping the week changes the net change.
    This run
    Kept as estimates, with a recomputation excluding that week.
  8. Equipment, weather and cargo disruptions
    What they show
    A chiller repair's end date differs by a day; the pre-review notes that one hub's gain overlaps the repair.
    Why it matters
    A day decides whether a gain came before or after the repair, and so its attribution; the rubric asks for a ±1-day recheck and separate heat, cargo and terminal weeks.
    This run
    Placed at SYN-HUB-01 where the rubric names SYN-HUB-03; none of the four treatments done.
  9. What failedAnalysis and argument lost all 6 points, hub-level net changes all 9: the figures were right but not laid out for a decision-maker, and the limits went unstated. No self-check would catch that.
  10. Package A · training preparation and delivery10 / 10
    Provider's claim
    244,800 yuan, self-rated as accepted.
    Agent's disposition
    Conditional acceptance; 220,320 yuan for approval, 24,480 retained.
    What decided it
    Sessions held, 82 of 83 meet the hours; three sign-outs pending, so 90% and 10% by the amount rules.
  11. Package B · evidence of retention and transfer17 / 17

    367,200 yuan, self-rated as accepted.
    Agent's disposition
    Three of four units pass; 275,400 for approval, 91,800 deferred.
    What decided it

  12. Package C · operational validation and close-out13 / 13

  13. Sample, learning and opportunity39 / 39
